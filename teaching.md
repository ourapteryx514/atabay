<section class="teaching-intro">

  <p class="teaching-lead">
    Alongside his research career, Professor Naz Mohammed Agh-Atabay
    contributed to chemistry education and graduate research training,
    particularly in synthetic, coordination, organometallic and
    macrocyclic chemistry.
  </p>

  <p>
    His teaching and supervision connected fundamental chemical concepts
    with laboratory synthesis, spectroscopic characterization and the
    investigation of molecular structure and chemical properties.
  </p>

</section>

  <div class="teaching-content">

    <h2>Teaching & Research Training</h2>

    <p>
      Professor Agh-Atabay's academic work at Fatih University combined
      university chemistry education with laboratory-based research.
      Students working under his supervision received training in
      synthesis, purification, structural characterization and the
      interpretation of spectroscopic data.
    </p>


    <div class="teaching-areas">

      <div class="teaching-area">

        <span class="teaching-number">01</span>

        <h3>Synthetic Chemistry</h3>

        <p>
          Preparation and characterization of organic ligands,
          macrocyclic compounds and coordination complexes.
        </p>

      </div>


      <div class="teaching-area">

        <span class="teaching-number">02</span>

        <h3>Coordination & Organometallic Chemistry</h3>

        <p>
          Metal–ligand chemistry, transition-metal complexes,
          coordination behaviour and organometallic systems.
        </p>

      </div>


      <div class="teaching-area">

        <span class="teaching-number">03</span>

        <h3>Spectroscopic Characterization</h3>

        <p>
          Structural investigation using techniques including FT-IR,
          FT-Raman, proton and carbon NMR and related analytical methods.
        </p>

      </div>


      <div class="teaching-area">

        <span class="teaching-number">04</span>

        <h3>Research Methodology</h3>

        <p>
          Experimental design, interpretation of chemical data,
          scientific literature and graduate research practice.
        </p>

      </div>

    </div>

  </div>

</section>
