<section class="teaching-intro">

  <p class="teaching-lead">
    Alongside his research career, Professor Naz Mohammed Agh-Atabay
    contributed to university chemistry education, laboratory teaching
    and graduate research training.
  </p>

  <p>
    His teaching and supervision connected synthetic chemistry with
    spectroscopy, structural characterization, transition-metal chemistry,
    macrocyclic and macromolecular systems and biological evaluation.
  </p>

</section>

    <div class="teaching-areas">

      <div class="teaching-area">
        <span class="teaching-number">01</span>
        <h3>Organic & Synthetic Chemistry</h3>
        <p>

        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="teaching-area">
        <span class="teaching-number">02</span>
        <h3>Coordination & Organometallic Chemistry</h3>

          synthesis and organometallic systems.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="teaching-area">
        <span class="teaching-number">03</span>
        <h3>Spectroscopic Characterization</h3>

          EPR and complementary analytical techniques.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="teaching-area">
