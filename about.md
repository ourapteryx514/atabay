<div class="about-intro">

  <p class="about-lead">
    <strong>Naz Mohammed Agh-Atabay</strong> is a retired professor of chemistry
    whose research career spans inorganic and coordination chemistry,
    organometallic chemistry, molecular materials, benzimidazole-based ligands,
    macrocyclic and heterocyclic compounds, spectroscopy, computational
    chemistry, and biologically active metal complexes.
  </p>

  <p>
    His scientific record extends from early work on transition-metal
    coordination compounds and organometallic systems to later research on
    functional ligands, macrocyclic molecules, antimicrobial compounds,
    molecular structure, and metal–ligand interactions. His work combines
    synthetic chemistry with structural and spectroscopic characterization,
    including NMR, FT-IR, FT-Raman, EPR, crystallographic methods, and
    computational approaches.
  </p>

</div>

<section class="about-block">

  <div class="about-label">
    RESEARCH
  </div>

  <div class="about-content">

    <h2>Evolution of the Research</h2>

    <p class="about-section-intro">
      The publication record shows a progression from fundamental inorganic
      and organometallic chemistry toward ligand design, molecular materials,
      spectroscopy, macrocyclic chemistry, computational studies, and
      biologically active compounds.
    </p>

    <div class="about-research-grid">


      <div class="about-research-item">
        <span>01</span>
        <h3>Coordination Chemistry</h3>
        <p>
          Early studies of molybdenum and tungsten complexes, Schiff-base
          coordination systems, phosphine and phosphine-oxide complexes,
          and transition-metal coordination chemistry.
        </p>
      </div>


      <div class="about-research-item">
        <span>02</span>
        <h3>Organometallic Chemistry</h3>
        <p>
          Molybdenum- and tungsten-mediated reactions of alkynes,
          η²-vinyl complexes, ethenethiolato systems, alkyne
          dimerisation and trimerisation, and the formation of novel
          metallacyclic structures.
        </p>
      </div>


      <div class="about-research-item">
        <span>03</span>
        <h3>Diacetylenes & Molecular Materials</h3>
        <p>
          Synthesis and characterization of diacetylene derivatives,
          metal-containing polydiacetylenes, topochemical polymerisation,
          and materials investigated for their optical properties.
        </p>
      </div>


      <div class="about-research-item">
        <span>04</span>
        <h3>Benzimidazole Ligand Chemistry</h3>
        <p>
          Design and synthesis of bisbenzimidazole and related chelating
          ligands, together with transition-metal complexes involving
          palladium, zinc, iron, mercury, cadmium, copper, and other metals.
        </p>
      </div>


      <div class="about-research-item">
        <span>05</span>
        <h3>Macrocyclic & Heterocyclic Chemistry</h3>
        <p>
          Development of macrocyclic and hetero-macromolecular systems
          containing aza, oxo, oxa, thia, amide, amine, and imine
          functionalities.
        </p>
      </div>


      <div class="about-research-item">
        <span>06</span>
        <h3>Spectroscopy & Molecular Structure</h3>
        <p>
          Structural investigations using NMR, FT-IR, FT-Raman, Raman,
          EPR, crystallographic techniques, vibrational assignments,
          and density-functional calculations.
        </p>
      </div>


      <div class="about-research-item">
        <span>07</span>
        <h3>Biologically Active Compounds</h3>
        <p>
          Evaluation of antimicrobial activity, cytotoxicity,
          DNA-binding behaviour, and related biological properties of
          newly synthesized ligands, macrocycles, and metal complexes.
        </p>
      </div>


      <div class="about-research-item">
        <span>08</span>
        <h3>Computational & Theoretical Chemistry</h3>
        <p>
          Quantum-mechanical, conformational, tautomeric, and
          density-functional studies used alongside experimental
          spectroscopy to investigate molecular structure and
          vibrational behaviour.
        </p>
      </div>

    </div>

    </a>

  </div>

</section>
