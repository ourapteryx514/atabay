<section class="about-intro">

  <p class="about-lead">
    Naz Mohammed Agh-Atabay is a retired Professor of Chemistry whose
    research career spans organometallic and coordination chemistry,
    polymer chemistry, benzimidazole-based ligands, macrocyclic compounds,
    spectroscopy, and biologically active metal complexes.
  </p>

  <p>
    His scientific work developed across several interconnected areas of
    inorganic, organometallic and synthetic chemistry. Early research
    concentrated particularly on transition-metal chemistry and
    metal-containing molecular and polymeric systems, while later work
    increasingly explored ligand design, coordination compounds,
    macrocyclic architectures and their biological properties.
  </p>

</section>

<section class="about-block">

  <div class="about-label">RESEARCH PATH</div>

  <div class="about-content">

    <h2>A Research Career Across Molecular Chemistry</h2>

    <div class="career-path">

      <div class="career-step">

        <span class="career-number">01</span>

        <div>
          <h3>Transition-Metal & Organometallic Chemistry</h3>

          <p>
            Early work investigated tungsten and molybdenum chemistry,
            including oxotungsten complexes, metal-mediated transformations
            of alkynes, vinyl and ethenethiolato complexes, and the formation
            and structural characterization of unusual metallacycles.
          </p>
        </div>

      </div>


      <div class="career-step">

        <span class="career-number">02</span>

        <div>
          <h3>Functional Molecular & Polymeric Systems</h3>

          <p>
            Research at Heriot-Watt University also extended into
            functionalized 1,3-diynes and metal-containing polydiacetylenes,
            combining synthetic chemistry with spectroscopic and optical
            characterization.
          </p>
        </div>

      </div>


      <div class="career-step">

        <span class="career-number">03</span>

        <div>
          <h3>Ligand & Coordination Chemistry</h3>

          <p>
            Later research focused strongly on benzimidazole-derived
            chelating ligands and their complexes with palladium, zinc,
            iron, cadmium, mercury and other transition-metal ions.
          </p>
        </div>

      </div>


      <div class="career-step">

        <span class="career-number">04</span>

        <div>
          <h3>Macrocyclic & Bioactive Chemistry</h3>

          <p>
            This work developed further into macrocyclic and
            cycloheterophane chemistry, antimicrobial activity,
            cytotoxicity, DNA binding, ion transport and relationships
            between molecular structure and biological function.
          </p>
        </div>

      </div>

    </div>

  </div>

</section>
